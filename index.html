What moves the price

No surprise line items.

  • System — uPVC or System Aluminium, and the profile series.
  • Size and span — larger glass and wider sliders need heavier sections.
  • Glass — single, toughened or double-glazed for noise.
  • Hardware and mesh — locks, handles, rollers and mosquito mesh.

Site measurement is free. Every item is written on the quote before anything is cut.

Is uPVC or System Aluminium better?

Neither is better everywhere. uPVC seals and insulates well for bedrooms and street-facing rooms. System Aluminium suits wide openings and slim sightlines. Many homes use both.

Will new windows actually cut traffic noise?

Most noise gets in through gaps, not the glass. A well-sealed frame with the right glass makes a clear difference; we’ll tell you honestly what to expect for your road.

Do you replace windows in an occupied home?

Yes. We remove the old frame, fit and seal the new one, and clean up the site — usually opening by opening, so the house stays usable.

How long does it take from visit to installation?

After you approve the written quote, we fabricate in our own workshop and give you an install date up front. We confirm the timeline at the site visit.

What happens after installation?

You get a direct number. If a sash needs adjusting or a lock feels stiff, you call a person who knows the job.
